    Findings

    Does the rich calc line fit without shrinking?
    Yes, trivially — because it never has to live in a 96px tile at all anymore. The reroll renders at the reveal list's full row width, so #167's d20-icon/boxed-total treatment needed zero adaptation from what RoundReveal already ships for layer 0. Now that it's also guaranteed to never carry a discarded die or effect badge (#219), it's an even simpler element than take 1 was squeezing in.
    Does the modal work for the tied player, and not get in a spectator's way?
    Yes in this fixture — "Viewing as" a tied player shows the modal immediately (blocking, matches how a tied player has no other useful action available); every other perspective (the other tied player, spectators) sees no modal, just the pending row updating live once the broadcast lands. That split maps cleanly onto the existing selfPlayerId-vs-others pattern TieBanner already uses today for showing the roll input only to the tied player it belongs to.
    Does the dependent-row connector read as "depends on the row above" at a glance?
    Reasonably — the indent plus a short corner-line under the avatar makes the relationship legible without a label. Decided: chained ties nest a further indented row, not replace the previous one in place — Rowan's row above demonstrates it, reveal level by level via the modal ("Viewing as" Rowan) or the debug reset. Keeps the whole tie-break history visible rather than losing the fact that a first reroll also tied.
    Is there still anything worth "rich" about a tie-break roll now?
    Decided: yes, keep the d20-icon/boxed-total treatment even though it can never carry a discarded die or effect badge — it's still a nicer read than bare text, and every reroll level in a chain gets it consistently.

    Decision

    1. The modal + dependent-row interaction model is confirmed — filed as issue #220, separable from the spell-effects question that originally motivated take 1, and now simpler to build since the reroll content itself is guaranteed plain.
    2. Chained ties nest indented rows (not in-place replacement) — see Rowan's fixture above.
    3. Keep the rich d20/boxed-total decoration for every reroll level, chained or not.
    4. Real implementation is still a genuine scope jump from a tile tweak: RoundReveal.tsx currently has no concept of layer>0 rolls or tie state at all — it'd need to grow both, plus a modal component and its own roll-submission flow separate from TieBanner's existing one. TieBanner/PlayerTile may end up retired for this call site entirely rather than adapted. Captured in #220.
    5. Depends on #219 shipping first — no point building the dependent-row plumbing against a data model that (until #219 lands) still lets a reroll carry a discarded die/effect badge it shouldn't.
